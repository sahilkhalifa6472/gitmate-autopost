Your expensive AI model should be the boss, not the intern 🧠

/improve is a new agent skill from shadcn (creator of shadcn/ui):
→ Your smartest model audits the whole repo: bugs, security, performance, tests, tech debt, deps, DX, docs + feature ideas
→ Every finding comes with exact file:line evidence, impact, effort and confidence
→ It re-checks every finding itself and drops the false alarms
→ You pick what matters, it writes one self-contained plan per finding in plans/
→ Plans have verification gates + STOP conditions, so a much cheaper model can execute them step by step
→ It never touches your source code. The plan is the product.

Install: npx skills add shadcn/improve
Then run: /improve (or /improve quick, /improve security, /improve branch)
