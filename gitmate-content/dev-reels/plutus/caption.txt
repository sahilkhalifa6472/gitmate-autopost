Expenses in one app, crypto in another, stocks in a messy spreadsheet? 😵‍💫

Plutus, built by @sebaptcd, is a free, open-source tracker that puts all your money in one place:
→ Business expenses, income and ledgers
→ Crypto, stocks, gold and cash, with live prices
→ Exchange rates locked on the day you traded, average-cost or FIFO profit
→ Private: no account, no sign-up, your data stays in your browser (optional encrypted sync)
→ Nearly 1,000 commits from a tiny team

Try it: just open plutus.inoculens.com in your browser.
