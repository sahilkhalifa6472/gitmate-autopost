The network is down. No internet, no signal. How do you text your family? 📵

DarkProof by @is4wiz is a messaging app for when there's no internet and no mobile network:
→ Phones talk to each other directly over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or a LoRa radio
→ Every phone passes messages along (up to 16 hops), so they reach people way out of range
→ End-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and signed: the phones in between can't read a word
→ "I need help" in one tap, check-ins with battery + position, offline street maps
→ An AI that runs right on your phone, no internet needed
→ No servers, no accounts. Android and iPhone, same format
