Still paying every month just to look at your own business's books? 🧾

Personal ERP, built by solo dev @madhur_772005, is a free, self-hosted ERP for small businesses:
→ Billing: money in / money out that posts real double-entry
→ Accounting: chart of accounts, journals, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow
→ Sales: quotations, orders, GST invoices, receivables
→ Purchasing + inventory with stock valuation
→ Invoice scanning: upload a bill, fields get extracted for you
→ Web app + desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux)

Runs on your own server, your data stays in your own PostgreSQL. No seat count, no renewal email.
Start: git clone → make setup → make up (one Docker Compose stack)
