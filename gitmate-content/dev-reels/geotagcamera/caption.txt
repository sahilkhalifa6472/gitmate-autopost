Every GPS camera app… ads before the camera even opens, then a subscription just to stamp your location? 📍

GeoTag Camera, built by @konkomaji, is a free, open-source GPS camera for Android:
→ Stamps place, address (Plus Code), coordinates and time on every photo
→ Signs each photo on your phone (SHA-256 + Android Keystore), so anyone can check the photo and its GPS/time weren't changed
→ Works with no signal: capture, stamp, sign and verify offline
→ Export CSV, KML, GeoJSON and PDF site reports
→ No ads, no account, no paywall

Install: download the APK from GitHub Releases (Android 8.0+, no Google Play services needed). F-Droid and Google Play coming soon.

Free, GPL-3.0 · built by @konkomaji 🙌
