# git.mate viral playbook

Read this BEFORE writing any git.mate reel script. Updated every Sunday by the weekly study task (owner rule 2026-11-15: "keep learning, become a viral machine").

## Rules (apply to EVERY reel, most of all to repos people DM'd us)
1. HOOK = A DAILY PAIN, NOT THE REPO NAME. First line (voice + big text, 0-2s) is a pain or question the viewer already has ("Why is Explorer STILL searching for a file you saved yesterday?"). The repo name comes at ~3-4s as the answer.
2. SHOW THE PAIN IN THE FIRST 2 SECONDS with real footage or a real screenshot of the problem (the slow app, the error, the bill, the mess), then the fix.
3. SPEAK TO NORMAL USERS, not only devs: say what it does for them in plain words. No jargon in the first 5 seconds.
4. "FREE REPLACEMENT FOR X YOU HATE" framing whenever it is true. Say "free" out loud and on screen.
5. EASIEST INSTALL: show the one-click way (store, download, one command). If it needs a terminal, show it as one short line.
6. SHOW THE REAL APP: real UI/README media for every feature, one feature per scene, 3-5 features.
7. BUILDER STORY: "built by a solo dev / a student / @user" in one line. For DM'd repos this is their story: who built it and why.
8. LENGTH under 35s. Cover frame must show the full pain hook.
9. Pick order for the trending reel: free apps normal people use daily (Windows/Mac/Android/browser/phone tools, replacements for paid or slow things) BEFORE niche AI-agent/CLI tools.

## Evidence
- 2026-11-15 study: Atlas #19 = 111.7k views (others 250-4.6k), avg watch 16s of 34.6s (others 6-9s), skip rate 28.1%, 5.7k saves, 2.2k shares, 611 comments, 1,221 follows, 99.3% non-followers, 86% from Reels tab. Audience India 36%, age 18-34 = 87%. Next best: Floci 4.6k, Sherlock 4.2k, ThinkRail 3.9k (all "instantly clear what it does for me").
