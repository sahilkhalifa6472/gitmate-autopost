Five different apps… just to download stuff? 😩

Ghost Downloader is a free, open-source download manager that does it all in one app:
→ Paste a video link, drag the timeline and save only the part you need
→ Torrents (magnet), live streams (M3U8), FTP, eD2k and Hugging Face models in one queue
→ Link expired mid-download? Change it, your progress is kept
→ Browser extension sends downloads straight to the app
→ Windows, macOS, Linux and Android
→ ~13.9K ⭐ on GitHub, GPL-3.0
