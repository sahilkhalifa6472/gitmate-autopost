Stop typing. Just talk, and your words land in any app. 🎙️

Handy (36k+ ⭐ on GitHub) is a free, open source speech-to-text app that runs completely offline:
→ Hold a shortcut, speak, let go: the text gets pasted right where your cursor is
→ Works in any text field: Slack, your code editor, Google Docs, anywhere
→ Local models: Whisper (small / medium / turbo / large) or Parakeet V3, which is CPU-optimized
→ Your voice never leaves your machine, no cloud, no account
→ Silence filtered with Silero VAD before transcription

✅ Free + open source (MIT)
✅ macOS, Windows and Linux (brew install --cask handy / winget install cjpais.Handy)
