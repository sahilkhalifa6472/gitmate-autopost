Your server's disk is at 95%... and you have no idea 😬

Infradeck by @karlo.ks is self-hosted monitoring for your homelab:
→ Every server, every container, every service in one dashboard
→ A tiny collector on each machine reports back over your VPN
→ Uptime checks run from your own machines, so you can tell a dead service from a broken network path
→ An optional AI incident agent reads masked evidence, explains what's wrong and proposes a fix. It never runs commands on your servers
→ Add a server with one command and one click

Docker Compose · open source (AGPL-3.0)
