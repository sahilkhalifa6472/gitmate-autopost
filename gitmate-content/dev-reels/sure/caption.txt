Sure is a free, open-source personal finance app you run yourself:
→ Net worth, cash flow, spending and outflows on one clean dashboard
→ Connect bank accounts, set budgets, track investments
→ Import your old Mint (or Monarch) data
→ Built-in AI assistant you can ask about your money
→ Self-host with one Docker compose file: your money data stays with you

The backstory: the Maybe Finance team spent nearly $1M building it, then open-sourced it. Sure is the community fork that kept it alive.
