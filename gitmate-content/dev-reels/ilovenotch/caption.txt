That black notch on your MacBook? It does… nothing. 🖤

ILoveNotch, built by @niyamvora, turns it into a tiny control center:
→ Hover it: now playing + a volume slider for every app
→ Drop files on it to keep them handy (Quick Look, AirDrop)
→ Calendar, Reminders, notes, timers, network speed
→ See your Claude, Codex and Copilot usage limits at a glance
→ Native SwiftUI, ~77 MB of RAM, 0.0% CPU at rest

Install: download the DMG, drag it into Applications, done (or brew install --cask niyamvora/tap/ilovenotch). macOS 14.6+.
