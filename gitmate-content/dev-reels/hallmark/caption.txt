You can spot an AI-made website in two seconds. Hallmark fixes that. ✍️

Hallmark is a free, open-source design skill for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor, made by Together AI:
→ Deals you 3 themes from a catalog of 48 real materials
→ Builds the page around what the visitor came to do
→ Lints it in a real browser against the "AI slop" tells (eyebrow labels, one colored word, a sign-up box on every page)
→ A fresh-context reviewer gets the last word
→ Redesign or audit any existing page by name

✅ One command: npx skills add nutlope/hallmark -g
✅ Free + MIT licensed
